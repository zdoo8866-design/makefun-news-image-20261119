1. **US, China, Russia summit** — Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen for the first trilateral summit, on the APEC sidelines; the three issue a joint statement on UN reform and nuclear dialogue. (AP / Reuters / NPR)
2. **Iran strikes Gulf bases** — Iranian drones hit US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain after US strikes on Qeshm Island. (BBC / Reuters / CBS)

4. **Voyager 1: one light-day away** — NASA confirms Voyager 1 (launched 1977) is the first human-made object one light-day from Earth, about 26 billion km; its radio signals now take 24 hours to arrive. The crossing was calculated for 18 Nov ET and confirmed/reported on 19 Nov, when the signal reached Earth. (AP via phys.org / Dawn / DD India)
5. **GTA VI launches worldwide** — Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI releases on 19 November for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, 13 years after GTA V. (USA Today / Le Monde / BusinessWorld)
6. **Van Gogh: $190.4M record** — "Châtaigniers en fleurs" (1890) sells at Sotheby's New York, a record for the artist (hammer fell on the New York evening of 18 Nov, i.e. 19 Nov UTC). (Reuters / The Art Newspaper)
