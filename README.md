## Revision note

Revised at the owner's direction, to his editorial standard: facts first, no exaggeration, no alarmist wording, no taking sides — plain labels that state what happened. Selection rebalanced for the full picture across regions and fields: the first draft had four war/conflict panels out of six; this draft keeps one direct-combat story (Iran–Gulf) and one court ruling (Myanmar), and adds the day's major science story (Voyager 1) and culture/tech story (GTA VI launch). ## Version 3 — "One Day on Earth"

The owner rejected the panel-grid drafts (v1, v2): the work had to be distinctive, and it had to carry his viewpoint without editorializing. V3 is a single scene drawn like an astronomical chart: one Earth under the dated title, the day's six events as fine-line annotations pinned to the planet, and Voyager 1 at the end of the dotted arc that gives the chart its scale — one light-day, about 26 billion km. The juxtaposition is the whole statement: a summit, a strike, a court ruling, a probe, a game launch and a painting, all on one day, all inside one light-day of home. Nothing in the image argues; it places the facts side by side and leaves judgment to the viewer. The painting in the gilt frame is the actual "Châtaigniers en fleurs" (1890; public domain, via Wikimedia Commons), perspective-set into the artwork. Design debts: the 24-hour-clock-around-Earth data-art tradition, inverted — one real day charted rather than deep time compressed — and the map-annotation graphics of award-winning news design (e.g. National Geographic's Society for News Design medal work).

## Version 4 — back to the grid, one shared backdrop

The owner rejected v3: as a pure text-annotation chart it explained nothing — no scenes, nothing graspable at a glance. His direction for v4: return to v2's six-panel grid of drawn scenes (each panel instantly shows its event), keep it simple, but put real detail inside the simplicity — every panel now carries a headline plus one concrete factual detail line (the first trilateral meeting and arms control; Qeshm Island; the 9–6 vote; ~26 billion km and the 24-hour signal; 13 years after GTA V; the painting's title and year). The innovation is modest and structural: all six panels are composed over ONE shared background — a single bright Shenzhen summit hall overlooking the skyline, the "one place" the whole day is viewed from — instead of six separate boxes on white; each panel also carries a small location tag (SHENZHEN · THE GULF · THE HAGUE · DEEP SPACE · LAUNCH NIGHT · NEW YORK), and a footer inventories the day without commentary: "A SUMMIT · A STRIKE · A RULING · A PROBE · A GAME · A PAINTING". Palette kept light and harmonious across all panels (the Gulf at pale lavender dusk, deep space in soft periwinkle, launch night in pastel pink). All type is typeset programmatically, so every word and number is exact.

## Version 5 — a little more detail on the three leaders

The owner approved v4 and asked for exactly one tweak: the people needed "a little bit more detail — not too lifelike, just a little bit of identifying features, so you can tell who is who. A little bit. Not too much." V5 changes only the summit panel's three figures, by targeted recoloring of the v4 artwork (everything else is pixel-identical to v4 — verified by diff): Trump gets blond swept-back hair and a bright red tie; Xi keeps his black side-parted hair and gets a dark red tie; Putin's hair becomes short, pale ash-blond (light/thinning) with a dark tie. Flat illustration throughout — color cues only, no facial likeness added. The auctioneer and crowds stay generic, as do all other panels, text, tags and the shared backdrop.

## Version 6 — different builds, bigger panels, one stat chip each

The owner approved v5's direction and asked for three final touches. First, the three summit leaders no longer share one build: by targeted warping of the summit scene (same method as v5's recolor — everything else in the scene is untouched, and Xi's area is pixel-identical), Trump is now slightly taller and broader, Xi keeps the medium build as the reference, and Putin is slightly shorter and slimmer — the same restrained "a little bit" register as the hair and ties, which are unchanged. Second, the panels are enlarged within the fixed canvas: margins and gutters trimmed (30/16 → 18/12 px) and the title block compressed, so the six scenes occupy more of the page while the shared Shenzhen backdrop still shows around them. Third, each panel gains one small stat chip in the location-pill style, using only facts already in the existing copy — 3 LEADERS · 2 BASES · 9–6 · 26 BN KM · 13 YEARS ON · $190.4M. No new facts, no extra sentences.
